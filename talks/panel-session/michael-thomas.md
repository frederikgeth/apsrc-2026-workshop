.. to fully realise the potential of interoperable open-source software, we suggest
growing and supporting a strong and enthusiastic energy modelling community. In
this workshop we will discuss the steps need to achieve interoperability in
open-source energy modelling tools and ask open questions on how we might go
about growing and supporting the Australian energy modelling community.
