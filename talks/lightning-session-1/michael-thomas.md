We are seeing research projects emerge that combine two or more actively
developed open-source energy modelling tools in fun and interesting ways.
However, combining these tools on an ad-hoc and manual basis is not only time
consuming, but minimises understandability, robustness and reusability. ACES
has developed an ecosystem of software packages, called the Energy Research
Information Stack (ERIS), to improve the interoperability of existing and
emerging open-source modelling software.
